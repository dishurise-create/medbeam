MedBeam Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

The short version: MedBeam has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We never ask for your name, prescription, doctor, diagnosis, insurance or medical history. If you choose to share whether a pharmacy had a medication, only the medication, the pharmacy's location, your answer and the time are shared, anonymously.

What stays on your iPhone

These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Your search history, saved medications and the last ZIP code you entered
  • A list of the reports you made (so you can see them in History)
  • Your settings (theme, colors, text size)
  • An optional Google API key, if you add one, stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain

You can delete all of this anytime in Settings → Delete all data on this iPhone, or by deleting the app.

Anonymous availability reports

When you tap "Yes, they had it" or "No, they didn't", MedBeam shares this with other users through our database (hosted by Supabase):

SharedExample
Medication ID (a standard government drug code)861226
The pharmacy's map location (not yours)38.93806, -77.55369
Your answerHad it / didn't have it
The time (set by the server)Sept 24, 2026, 7:38 PM

Reports contain no name, account, device ID or location of you, and cannot be linked back to you. "Prefer not to answer" shares nothing. Like most internet services, our database provider may briefly keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security; we do not store IP addresses with reports and do not use them to identify anyone.

Because reports are anonymous, we cannot find or delete a specific person's report. Reports older than 45 days are no longer shown.

Services MedBeam uses to look things up

To show results, the app sends your search (not your identity) to these services:

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
Apple MapsThe ZIP code or map area you searchFind nearby pharmacies
National Library of Medicine (RxNorm)The medication name you typeIdentify the exact medication and strength
U.S. FDA (openFDA)Medication codesOfficial shortage, product and recall data
Medicaid (data.medicaid.gov)Medication product codesPharmacy price trends
Google Places (only if you add your own API key)The map area you searchPharmacy ratings and hours

These are separate services with their own privacy policies. MedBeam does not use GPS location tracking; it only uses the ZIP code you type or the map area you choose.

What we never do

  • Sell or share personal information (we don't collect any)
  • Show ads or use advertising trackers
  • Track you across other apps or websites
  • Give medical advice. MedBeam is an information tool; always talk to your doctor or pharmacist about your medication.

Children

MedBeam is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
